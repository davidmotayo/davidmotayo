# 👋 Hello, I'm David Ogunrinola

### GRC Analyst | Cybersecurity & Information Security Compliance Professional


I'm a Computer Science graduate (Caleb University, Lagos, 2025) working at the intersection of **Governance, Risk & Compliance** and **software development**. I build ISMS documentation, risk registers, and compliance frameworks the way an engineer builds software — structured, evidence-backed, and grounded in how systems actually work under the hood.

My focus is ISO/IEC 27001 & 27701 implementation, risk management (NIST CSF, IEC 62443), and Nigerian regulatory compliance (NDPA 2023, CBN Cybersecurity Framework). Because I also come from a web development background, I bring a technical fluency to GRC work that's often missing from purely policy-driven practitioners — I understand the systems I'm assessing, not just the controls that govern them.

### 🛡️ Core Competencies


- **ISMS Implementation:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022 & 27701 — risk registers, Statements of Applicability, Annex A control mapping, internal audit findings, risk treatment plans.
- **Risk Management Frameworks:** NIST SP 800-39 / 800-37, ISO 31000, NIST CSF, IEC 62443 (OT/industrial control systems).
- **Regulatory Compliance:** NDPA 2023 (Nigeria Data Protection Act), CBN Cybersecurity Framework, HIPAA, PCI DSS, SOC 2 mapping for cross-sector scenarios.
- **Quantitative Risk Analysis:** Single/Annualized Loss Expectancy (SLE/ALE) modeling for risk prioritization.
- **Governance Models:** Three Lines of Defence, security governance planning, executive compliance reporting.
- **Security Documentation:** Policy authoring (password & access control with RBAC), audit checklists, treatment plans.
